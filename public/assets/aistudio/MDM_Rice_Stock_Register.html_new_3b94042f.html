How to use

  1. Choose month and year, enter the actual rice stock on the 1st, press Update.
  2. Set rice per child (kg) for each section once.
  3. Attendance comes from the Master Attendance sheet. Type rice received directly in the Rice received column.
  4. Opening, consumption and closing are calculated automatically; each day opens with the previous day's closing.
  5. Data is stored in this browser only. Use Print for the monthly register.
